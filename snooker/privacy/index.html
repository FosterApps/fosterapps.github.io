    Snooker Scoreboard Scorer – Privacy Policy

    Last updated: October 12, 2026

    Summary

    Snooker Scoreboard Scorer is designed to respect your privacy. The app scores your matches locally on your device and stores match history using on-device storage. Snooker Scoreboard Scorer does not create user accounts and does not collect personal information such as your name or email. It sends only the anonymous usage and error reports described below under Analytics & Diagnostics.

    Data collection

    What Snooker Scoreboard Scorer collects

    Snooker Scoreboard Scorer is developed and operated by Foster Apps LLC. The app stores player names, frame scores, breaks, and match results locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server.

    Snooker Scoreboard Scorer does not:

    • Collect or store your real name, email address, or any personally identifying information
    • Transmit any match data you enter to external servers
    • Track your location
    • Use advertising identifiers or build advertising profiles
    • Sell or share your data with third parties
    Purchases

    In-App Purchases

    Snooker Scoreboard Scorer offers optional Pro subscriptions (Monthly and Yearly) processed entirely through Apple's App Store, unlocking matches longer than best of 5, handicap frames, and unlimited match history. Foster Apps does not receive or store your payment information. Purchase history is managed by Apple. See Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

    Analytics & Diagnostics

    Anonymous usage and error reports

    Snooker Scoreboard Scorer sends a small number of anonymous events to a Foster Apps service, such as the first time the app is opened, when you reach the paywall, when a purchase completes, and when an error occurs. Error reports include the error message, technical details of where it happened, and the app version. Events are tied only to a random identifier created when you install the app. They contain no name, email, advertising identifier, location, or the content you create in the app, and they are used only to understand how the app is used and to fix bugs.

    Crash Reporting

    Diagnostics

    If you have enabled "Share with App Developers" in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may provide anonymised crash reports to Foster Apps. These reports contain no personally identifying information and are used solely to fix bugs.

    Children

    Children's privacy

    Snooker Scoreboard Scorer does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app contains no advertising, no account creation, and no social features.

    Contact

    Questions

    If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at privacy@fosterapps.com.
