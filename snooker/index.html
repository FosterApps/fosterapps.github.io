    Built for the table.

    Keep up with a real frame — score by hand, fast, with zero fuss.

    RULES

    The Table Knows What's On

    Red, then any colour, then red again — then the colours in order. 6, 10 or 15 reds. Balls that can't be played are greyed out, so a stray tap can't wreck a frame.

    BREAKS

    Every Break Counted

    Live break, points left on the table, snookers required, and each player's highest break and centuries, frame by frame — with frame and match timers.

    FOULS

    Fouls and Free Ball

    Foul values from 4 to 7 awarded to your opponent, reds potted on the foul stroke removed from the table, and free ball when you're snookered.

    UNDO

    Undo Anything

    Step back through every shot — even after the frame has ended. Plus the re-spotted black when the scores finish level.

    SHARE

    Share the Result

    Send the frame or match result as an image, and read the score from across the room in the landscape scoreboard view. The screen stays awake while you score.

    REMATCH

    One-Tap Rematch

    Run it back with the same players and rules in one tap — your regulars are remembered for next time.
