Frequently asked questions

How does a spirit box work?

A spirit box scans through radio static, and some people believe spirits can shape that static into words. Spirit Box recreates the experience with a continuous, never-repeating AM-radio soundscape and procedurally generated words that arrive on their own or in response to your voice — entertainment, not a real communication device.

Is Spirit Box a real ghost detector?

No. Spirit Box is an entertainment app. Every word, spike, and spirit board answer is procedurally generated for entertainment purposes, not evidence of paranormal activity. The EMF meter reads your device's real magnetometer, but a magnetometer responds to ordinary magnetic sources like electronics and metal, not spirits.

What is EVP (electronic voice phenomenon)?

EVP refers to sounds or words some people report hearing in static or ambient audio, often interpreted as spirit communication. Spirit Box's ghost-box mode recreates the EVP session format — continuous scanning static layered with words — as a procedurally generated entertainment experience.

How do I use the spirit board in the app?

Open the Board tab (Pro), ask your question out loud, and watch the on-screen planchette drift letter by letter to spell out its answer — never revealed up front, only as it's spelled. It's a classic Ouija-style board built for a phone screen.

Does the EMF meter use my phone's real sensor?

Yes. The EMF tab reads your device's actual built-in magnetometer in real time, with baseline calibration and a disturbance log — it's a real sensor reading, not a simulated animation. It responds to ordinary magnetic sources like electronics and metal, not paranormal activity.
