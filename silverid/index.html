    Frequently asked questions

    How do I identify a silver hallmark?

    Photograph the mark as close and clear as your camera allows — hallmarks are small, so good lighting and a steady hand matter more than a fancy camera. Silver Hallmark Identifier reads the photo with AI and matches it against a verified library of purity stamps and assay office marks, returning a purity, likely origin, and a plain-language history of the mark.

    What does a 925 stamp mean on silver?

    925 means sterling silver: 92.5% pure silver alloyed with 7.5% other metal, usually copper, for strength. It's the most common silver standard worldwide and is often stamped alongside a country's own assay marks.

    Is an AI hallmark scan the same as a professional appraisal?

    No. Silver Hallmark Identifier gives an AI-generated read to help you understand a mark quickly, not a certified appraisal. For insurance, resale, or authentication purposes, a professional assayer or appraiser should verify anything valuable.

    Can I look up a hallmark without scanning it?

    Yes. The Mark Reference tab holds every purity stamp and assay office mark in the app's verified library, searchable and filterable, so you can browse and learn even when you don't have a piece in hand to scan.

    Does silver hallmark identification work on worn or partial marks?

    It can, but confidence drops with wear. Silver Hallmark Identifier shows a confidence level with every result — Low, Medium, or High — and flags uncertain reads as a possible match rather than presenting a guess as certain.
