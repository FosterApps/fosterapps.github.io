Silver Hallmark Identifier – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Summary

Silver Hallmark Identifier is designed to respect your privacy. Your scan photos, results, and history stay on your device. Silver Hallmark Identifier does not run its own servers, does not create user accounts, and does not sell or share your data with third parties. To generate a scan result, your photo is sent to a third-party AI vision provider for one-time analysis, as described below.

Data collection

What Silver Hallmark Identifier collects

Silver Hallmark Identifier is developed and operated by Foster Apps LLC. The app stores your scan photos, identification results, notes, and history on your device only.

Silver Hallmark Identifier does not:

  • Sell your data.
  • Track you across other apps or websites.
  • Use third-party advertising SDKs.
  • Require an account or email address.
  • Store your photos on any Foster Apps server.
AI Identification

How photo scanning works

When you scan a photo, the image (and any optional context notes you add, e.g. "inside of a spoon handle") is sent directly to a third-party AI vision provider (Google Gemini) for one-time analysis to identify the likely purity, origin, and history of the hallmark. The photo is processed to generate your result and is not stored by Foster Apps on any server. Use of the AI provider is subject to that provider's own privacy terms.

Silver Hallmark Identifier is a reference and identification tool, not an authentication or appraisal service. Results are AI-generated estimates for informational purposes only and are not a substitute for a professional assayer or appraiser.

Storage

On-device storage

Your scan photos, results, notes, and history are stored locally on your device using on-device storage. This data is not synced to iCloud or any Foster Apps server. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data will permanently remove it.

Subscriptions

In-app purchases

Silver Hallmark Identifier Pro is available as a subscription through Apple's App Store. All billing is handled by Apple. Silver Hallmark Identifier never sees your payment details. Subscription management and cancellation are handled through your Apple ID settings.

Report Export

Scan reports

When you export a scan report (Pro), it is generated entirely on your device. Silver Hallmark Identifier does not upload your data to any server to produce the report. Sharing is handled by iOS's system share sheet — the data goes only to the destination you choose.

Support

Support email

When you email support at support+silverid@fosterapps.com, the contents of your email are stored in your email provider's systems.

Changes

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Silver Hallmark Identifier evolves. When it changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of the page will be revised. Significant changes may also be mentioned in release notes.
