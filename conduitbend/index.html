    Built for the bender.

    Quick answers on the job, with the math there when you want to check it.

    MARKS

    Marks in Fractions

    Type 6 1/2 and get a tape-measure answer. Bender-chart multipliers by default, exact trig when you want it, inches or millimeters.

    STEPS

    Steps for Every Bend

    Numbered instructions, including which way to flip the pipe, plus a Show the math view so you can check every number.

    RUNS

    Plan the Whole Stick

    Chain bends together and get every mark from the end of the pipe, the total shrink, the cut length and a 360 degree bend counter.

    RACKS

    Parallel Offsets

    Stagger the marks for a whole rack of conduit, with mixed sizes spaced by their real outside diameters.

    LEVEL

    Phone Protractor

    Lay your phone along the bent leg to check the angle, with a tick when you hit a standard bend.
