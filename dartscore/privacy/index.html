    Darts Scorer – Privacy Policy

    Last updated: September 28, 2026

    Summary

    Darts Scorer is designed to respect your privacy. The app scores your matches locally on your device and stores match history using on-device storage. Darts Scorer does not run its own servers, does not create user accounts, and does not collect or transmit personal information.

    Data collection

    What Darts Scorer collects

    Darts Scorer is developed and operated by Foster Apps LLC. The app stores player names, match scores, checkout history, and averages locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server.

    Darts Scorer does not:

    • Collect or store your real name, email address, or any personally identifying information
    • Transmit any match data you enter to external servers
    • Track your location
    • Use advertising identifiers or build advertising profiles
    • Sell or share your data with third parties
    Purchases

    In-App Purchases

    Darts Scorer offers optional Pro subscriptions (Monthly and Yearly) processed entirely through Apple's App Store, unlocking Cricket mode and unlimited match history. Foster Apps does not receive or store your payment information. Purchase history is managed by Apple. See Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

    Crash Reporting

    Diagnostics

    If you have enabled "Share with App Developers" in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may provide anonymised crash reports to Foster Apps. These reports contain no personally identifying information and are used solely to fix bugs.

    Children

    Children's privacy

    Darts Scorer does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app contains no advertising, no account creation, and no social features.

    Contact

    Questions

    If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at privacy@fosterapps.com.
